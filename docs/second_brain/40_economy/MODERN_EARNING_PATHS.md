# Modern Earning Paths

## Design Goal

Move beyond old asset categories. The game world should include modern ways people try to make money: creator economy, AI tools, vibe-coding, mini-apps, communities, small service businesses, bots, and platform risk.

## Earning Categories

### Creator

- Blog.
- YouTube-like channel.
- Short video page.
- Paid community.
- Niche course.
- Newsletter.

Risks: algorithm change, burnout, sponsor scandal, equipment expense, reputation shock.

### Builder

- Mini-app.
- Vibe-coded SaaS.
- Telegram bot.
- Automation script.
- No-code marketplace product.

Risks: platform API change, security bug, support overload, competitor clone, hosting bill spike.

### Operator

- Repair garage.
- Cleaning team.
- Delivery route.
- Small production.
- Local service team.

Risks: employee churn, equipment failure, tax inspection, customer demand swing.

### Investor/Speculator

- Fictional stocks.
- Fictional crypto.
- Futures.
- Fund/broker mechanic.

Risks: market shock, liquidation, trust collapse, regulation.

### Expert/Service

- Accountant.
- Stylist.
- Mechanic.
- Analyst.
- Prompt engineer.
- Ad buyer.

Risks: certification cost, reputation, client churn, skill decay.

## Expense-To-Asset Rule

Some expenses are not dead money. They become latent assets.

Examples:

- Welding machine -> garage synergy.
- Stylist course -> creator/fashion business synergy.
- Camera kit -> content income boost.
- Coding bootcamp -> mini-app launch discount.
- Old van -> delivery route boost.
- 3D printer -> prototype/product card unlock.

This creates the emotional lesson: bad-looking spending can become useful if it matches a future opportunity.

## Staff Market

Staff appear in a rotating job market:

- Basic assistant.
- Advanced assistant.
- Star assistant.
- Accountant.
- Mechanic.
- Content editor.
- AI agent operator.
- Lawyer.
- Tax consultant.
- Ad buyer.

Each staff card has monthly cost, slot effect, skill tag, loyalty/reliability, training path, and event vulnerabilities.

## Suggested MVP Staff

- Basic assistant: 500/month, +2 small business slots.
- Advanced assistant: 1000/month, +3 slots, chance of +50 percent output on one business.
- Star assistant: 1500/month, +5 slots, chance that 2 businesses double output.
- Accountant: 700/month, reduces audit damage and reveals tax-risk cards earlier.
- AI operator: 800/month, reduces launch cost for mini-app/automation cards.

# Economy and Markets

## Economy Pillars

- Cashflow beats vanity wealth.
- Liquidity matters.
- Leverage accelerates both wins and failures.
- Relationships create optionality.
- Safe boring choices should sometimes be optimal.

## Asset Classes

| Class | MVP Examples | Risk |
|-------|--------------|------|
| Deposit | Bank lock, promo rate | Inflation, opportunity cost |
| Small business | Kiosk, online course, repair team | Upkeep, slot usage |
| Big business | Franchise, logistics hub | Large debt, 2 slots |
| Real estate-like | Rental pod, storage unit | Vacancy, repair |
| Crypto spot | Fictional tokens | Volatility |
| Futures | 2x/3x synthetic contracts | Liquidation |
| Assistants | Operator, analyst, negotiator | Subscription |
| Trading bots | Grid bot, risk bot | Malfunction, fees |

## Market Pulse Deck

Events should affect multiple strategies:

- Liquidity crunch.
- Creator economy boom.
- Regulation rumor.
- Energy price shock.
- Bank promo week.
- AI tools productivity wave.
- Exchange outage.
- Consumer demand spike.

## Balancing Hypothesis

Win strategies:

- Safe accumulator: deposits + low-risk businesses + low debt.
- Deal maker: co-investments + buyouts + reputation.
- Speculator: futures/crypto, high upside, frequent liquidation risk.
- Operator: assistants + business slots + recurring income.

No strategy should exceed 35 percent win rate in bot simulations after tuning.

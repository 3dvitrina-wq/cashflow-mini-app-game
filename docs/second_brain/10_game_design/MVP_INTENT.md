# MVP Intent

## Working Pitch

A Telegram-native financial strategy table where 2-6 players try to escape fragile income by building resilient cashflow, negotiating deals, surviving market shocks, and choosing between safe compounding and risky acceleration.

## Target Audience

- Core: 18-35, Telegram-native, interested in money, crypto, entrepreneurship, self-development, games.
- Secondary: 30-45, business communities, investment clubs, education events.
- Not for children in MVP because leverage/crypto/futures mechanics require risk framing.

## MVP Match

- 2-6 players.
- 25-45 minutes ranked, 10-15 minutes tutorial sprint.
- Mobile portrait first.
- Turn timer: 45-90 seconds depending on phase.
- Win by financial freedom score: passive net cashflow + net worth + resilience + goal completion.

## What Makes It Special

- AI host as a charismatic table master, but not a fragile dependency.
- Deals between players are structured and fast.
- Player who leaves is replaced by a bot, so the table survives.
- Fictional crypto/futures and trading bots exist, but the game teaches margin and liquidation risk.
- Boring choices can win: deposits, diversification, insurance, low-risk businesses.
- Non-obvious deals become great only for the right player at the right time.

## MVP Screens

- Bot landing and invite.
- Lobby with seats and readiness.
- Player dashboard: cash, income, expenses, assets, liabilities, risk, slots.
- Deal/card screen.
- Market ticker.
- Negotiation modal.
- AI host feed.
- Endgame recap and challenge/rematch.

## Explicit Non-Goal

MVP is not a 3-4 hour online reproduction of an offline Cashflow session with a live video host. Human-hosted long games can become premium events later, but the core product must fit Telegram behavior: short, restartable, social, and playable without scheduling a webinar-sized block of life.

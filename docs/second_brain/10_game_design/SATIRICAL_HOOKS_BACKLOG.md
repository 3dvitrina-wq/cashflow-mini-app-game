# Satirical Hooks Backlog

## Purpose

Parking lot for strong satirical mechanics that fit DYOR but should be selected carefully for MVP.

## Hooks

### Financial Influencers

- Guru promises x10.
- Closed rich club.
- "Millionaire mindset marathon."
- Signal channel with suspicious confidence.

Gameplay: may give motivation/networking, but often drains cash, raises FOMO, or damages reputation.

### Pseudo-Education

- "Earn with AI in 3 days."
- "Crypto for tired parents."
- "Passive income without passive income."
- "Course with 400 slides and one useful sentence."

Gameplay: sometimes gives skill tag, sometimes just PDF and shame.

### Banking Magic

- Credit limit appears when player is weak.
- Rate increases after player relies on debt.
- Bank asks for documents after approving everything.
- "Personal solution" is just a worse loan with nicer colors.

### Subscription Death

- SaaS tools.
- AI tools.
- Trading bots.
- Fitness, courses, cloud hosting.
- "Only 9.99" becomes cashflow leak.

Event: player finally opens bank statement.

### Family and Friends Pressure

- Brother asks for business loan.
- Friend invites to "one theme."
- Parents recommend apartment at the top.
- Relative knows a lawyer.

### Closed Chats and Communities

- VIP insider chat.
- Panic investor group.
- Founder mastermind.
- Signals with too many rocket emojis.

Gameplay: can provide early clue or herd madness.

### Personal Brand

Player can become an expert before understanding the topic.

Pros:

- creator income;
- deal access;
- influence.

Cons:

- reputation crash if public prediction fails;
- stress;
- scandal.

### Digital Nomad Absurdity

- Ocean view, blocked card.
- Visa run.
- Coworking without internet.
- Cheap rent, expensive paperwork.
- Timezone destroys deal window.

### Fake Stability

Job feels safe until:

- optimization;
- KPI surprise;
- new manager;
- "we are family";
- company pivot.

### Corporate Weirdness

- 360 review.
- mandatory fun;
- useless meeting;
- team-building;
- performance improvement plan.

Gameplay: drains energy and raises quit temptation.

### Credit Flex

- Car/phone/watch on credit.
- Gives negotiation/status bonus.
- Hurts cashflow.
- Can become shame or leverage in social deals.

### Tax Office As Boss

Tax office is nearly a character:

- "Tax Office Wakes Up."
- "Where is the contract?"
- "Camera audit."
- "Congratulations, you invented paperwork."

Lawyer/accountant become superheroes.

### Micro-Betrayals

- delayed payout;
- hidden risk;
- partial repayment;
- silent exit before crash;
- "forgot" to mention guarantee.

Social meatgrinder should include small betrayals, not only huge scams.

### Reputation Titles

- Cardboard Magnate.
- Futures Romantic.
- Serial Guarantor.
- Human Receipt.
- Almost Lawyer.
- Trust Issues Premium.

### Anti-Achievements

- sold safe deposit to 3x long;
- divorced into negative net worth;
- hired accountant after court;
- became guru before profit;
- went bankrupt with premium avatar.

### Family Council

Event where family blocks a reckless decision:

- cannot sell home for fictional token;
- spouse requires lawyer contract;
- child asks why food budget is in futures.

### FOMO Events

- "Everyone already made money."
- "Last chance."
- "Still early."
- "Only smart people understand this."

Higher stress increases chance to take bad FOMO action.

### AI Advisor Satire

In-game AI agent confidently makes wrong suggestion if player over-trusts automation.

Important: this is an in-game event, not actual AI host hallucination.

### Bureaucratic Quest

Open business requires documents:

- one expired;
- one signed by wrong person;
- one exists only on Tuesday;
- stamp needed from office that moved.

Lawyer can shortcut.

### Public Failure

Player posts "I quit my job forever" and later tries to return.

AI host should not stay quiet.

## Best Shortlist

Most promising for MVP/early packs:

1. FOMO and influencers.
2. Tax office / lawyers / paperwork.
3. Subscription death and AI tools.
4. Reputation / shame / anti-achievements.
5. Job absurdity and resignation.

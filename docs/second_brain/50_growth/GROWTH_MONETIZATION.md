# Growth and Monetization

## Engagement Hooks

- One-tap rematch.
- Challenge a ranked player.
- Invite Telegram chat to watch/join.
- Post-match recap card.
- Rivalry streak.
- Weekly scenario.
- Seasonal leagues.
- Achievements for behavior, not only winning.

## Achievement Ideas

- `Boring Genius`: win with no leverage.
- `Debt Surgeon`: remove 80 percent liabilities.
- `Calm Hands`: survive three market shocks.
- `Operator`: earn most income from businesses.
- `Syndicate`: close three co-investment deals.
- `Phoenix`: recover from liquidation and place top 2.
- `Mentor`: explain a deal to a new player.
- `Liquidity Left The Chat`: lose a futures trade to slippage.
- `Trust Issues`: betray investors and still survive the match.
- `Actually Useful Course`: turn a weird expense into a profitable synergy.
- `Vibe Shipper`: launch a mini-app with AI/operator support.
- `Adult Supervision`: hire an accountant before tax audit.

## Ranking

- Separate casual and ranked.
- Ranked uses match result plus risk-adjusted score.
- Leaderboard supports `Challenge` button.
- Challenge opens Telegram invite flow.

## Monetization

Allowed direction:

- Telegram Stars for digital goods.
- Host skins/voices.
- Cosmetic table themes.
- Premium private rooms.
- Tournament tickets.
- Season pass with education missions.
- Sponsor rooms for business communities.
- Club subscriptions.

Forbidden direction:

- Pay for better cards.
- Pay to undo liquidation in ranked.
- Pay to hide risk.
- Pay to target weaker players.

## Funnel

1. Friend sends match recap or challenge.
2. User opens Mini App in Telegram.
3. Tutorial sprint with bot table.
4. First casual match.
5. Achievement and recap.
6. Challenge/rematch/chat invite.
7. Ranked unlock after 2 finished matches.

## Social Triggers

- Player can challenge someone from rating.
- Player can invite Telegram chat to vote on next room volatility.
- Player can share a funny recap title.
- Players can form temporary fund/syndicate in chaos/private mode.
- Trust/reputation creates social memory: people remember who paid out and who did not.

## Room Mode Hooks

- Calm rooms are good onboarding.
- Normal rooms feed ranked.
- Financial Rollercoaster rooms create shareable chaos.
- Chaos rooms are best for friend groups, betrayal mechanics, and funny recaps.

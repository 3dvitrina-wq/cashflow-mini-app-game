## Decisions

- Futures should be designed as satirical risk theater: tempting, animated, laggy, funny, and statistically hostile to the player.
- Assistants are a major strategic layer: hired staff consume cashflow but unlock business slots, risk control, audits, and scaling.
- Trust/reputation should be mechanical, not just cosmetic; players can scam via fund/broker mechanics, but status consequences persist.
- Modern earning paths should include blogs, YouTube-like channels, vibe-coding, mini-apps, AI agencies, marketplaces, and creator tools.
- Crisis should be playable: avatar states, cardboard-box humor, room rental, relocation, help, co-owned property, and betrayal options.
- Stress, insurance/legal protection, reputation, epoch packs, post-match recap, and room modes are explicit systems, not loose flavor.
- Family, marriage, divorce, pets, bankruptcy abuse, guarantees, and deal paperwork are optional social-life systems with strict mode gates.

## Risks found

- Futures comedy must not become real gambling UX.
- Scam mechanics must be framed as social simulation with consequences, not endorsement.
- Too many professions can bloat MVP; start with a small typed catalog and expand by packs.
- Co-ownership betrayal can become toxic if allowed in all modes; likely restrict harsh betrayal to chaos/private modes.
- Player-to-player marriage/divorce must be opt-in and mode-gated to avoid creepy or hostile ranked behavior.

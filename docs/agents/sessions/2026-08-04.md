## Honest transport feedback

The WebSocket client already reconnected and resumed a room, but store actions
often ignored `send() === false` and immediately returned success. The UI could
therefore say an asset was bought, a deal accepted or a reaction sent when no
command left the phone.

Boolean economy operations now propagate transport failure. Personal offers,
table help, futures, incoming deals and card submission no longer claim success
on a dead socket. The main decision CTA blocks during reconnect, asks the player
to repeat the decision, and triggers an immediate reconnect. Stale financial
commands are intentionally not queued: replaying a purchase or sale after the
state changes would create duplicate or outdated decisions.

## Read-only futures math audit

The core direction formula is correct: notional is `margin × leverage`; long P&L
is `(mark − entry) × notional / entry`, short reverses the price delta. The
surrounding settlement contract is not ready to trust:

- one-round price movement cannot reach the current liquidation threshold, so
  liquidation warnings are effectively decorative;
- just before liquidation an ordinary loss can exceed margin, then become
  smaller when the next worse tick triggers liquidation;
- all-in margin hides cash from negative monthly flow and funding, producing an
  exploitable wallet result while the event log claims the fee was paid;
- the client receives deterministic RNG state and can predict the next server
  tick; the decorative chart is unrelated to the authoritative price;
- UI reports gross P&L while the wallet receives net P&L, and card-opened versus
  futures-screen positions use different liquidation rules.

This audit made no futures changes. Fix the settlement ledger and exact tests as
a separate engine task; do not tune win rates until accounting is internally
consistent and visible to the player.

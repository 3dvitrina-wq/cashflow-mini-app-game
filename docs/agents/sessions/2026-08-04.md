## Honest transport feedback

The WebSocket client already reconnected and resumed a room, but store actions
often ignored `send() === false` and immediately returned success. The UI could
therefore say an asset was bought, a deal accepted or a reaction sent when no
command left the phone.

Boolean economy operations now propagate transport failure. Personal offers,
table help, futures, incoming deals and card submission no longer claim success
on a dead socket. The main decision CTA blocks during reconnect, asks the player
to repeat the decision, and triggers an immediate reconnect. Stale financial
commands are intentionally not queued: replaying a purchase or sale after the
state changes would create duplicate or outdated decisions.

## Browser-discovered engine/UI mismatch

The 402×874 rendered market enabled Coffee at `$1,000`, but clicking it produced
`Недостаточно наличных` while the player had `$2,000+`. The real reason was a
canonical payload rejection: the registry requires upkeep `$10`, `$20` and `$50`
for Coffee, Kiosk and Studio, while the market omitted upkeep and sent zero.

The market now sends the complete canonical payload. Browser replay proved:

- Coffee purchase reduced cash by `$1,000`;
- the asset appeared in the profile ledger;
- passive income increased by `$200` and upkeep by `$10`;
- the market displays asset flow as `+$190/мес` with the `+$200 −$10`
  breakdown;
- sale review showed `+$720` cash and `−$190/мес` asset flow.

## Read-only futures math audit

The core direction formula is correct: notional is `margin × leverage`; long P&L
is `(mark − entry) × notional / entry`, short reverses the price delta. The
surrounding settlement contract is not ready to trust:

- one-round price movement cannot reach the current liquidation threshold, so
  liquidation warnings are effectively decorative;
- just before liquidation an ordinary loss can exceed margin, then become
  smaller when the next worse tick triggers liquidation;
- all-in margin hides cash from negative monthly flow and funding, producing an
  exploitable wallet result while the event log claims the fee was paid;
- the client receives deterministic RNG state and can predict the next server
  tick; the decorative chart is unrelated to the authoritative price;
- UI reports gross P&L while the wallet receives net P&L, and card-opened versus
  futures-screen positions use different liquidation rules.

This audit made no futures changes. Fix the settlement ledger and exact tests as
a separate engine task; do not tune win rates until accounting is internally
consistent and visible to the player.

## Additional Studio/process notes

- UI audits should first produce an attention-owner matrix: surface type,
  priority, safe-area rule, focus owner, Back/Escape behavior and what must pause
  underneath. This exposed more defects than reviewing z-index values alone.
- Reuse one modal primitive and one notice lane as measurable contracts; flag any
  new fixed overlay that bypasses them during preflight.
- For browser work, retain one mobile tab and take a scoped accessibility snapshot
  only after a state change. Geometry can be checked in one bounded DOM query;
  repeated full snapshots and build asset manifests consume context without
  improving confidence.
- Keep economy audit separate from visual audit, but record the join point
  (`UI claim → command → authoritative ledger → feedback`). This prevented a
  correct-looking futures screen from being mistaken for correct accounting.
- Studio polling was repaired after the measurement: DIOR now sends bounded run
  summaries instead of duplicated task prompts/results, refreshes CLI/Git
  diagnostics in the background and serves a stale complete snapshot while a
  refresh runs. Cold state dropped from about `885 KB / 18–23s` to
  `257 KB / 1.6s`; refreshed polls measured `278 KB / 6–18ms`. The July task was
  preserved as `stopped` with an append-only cancellation event, so resuming the
  workspace cannot launch obsolete work.
